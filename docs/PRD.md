# NubeNxt Website PRD

## 1. Objectives

- Position NubeNxt as a premium technology service provider.
- Clearly explain what NubeNxt does within a few seconds.
- Generate qualified business enquiries.
- Showcase NubeNxt's website work through prototypes/projects.
- Demonstrate premium design, readability and execution quality.

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary

Established businesses that already invest in:

- Branding
- Business premises
- Equipment
- Customer experience
- Professional presentation

### Initial Focus

1. Aesthetic / Dermatology Clinics
2. Cosmetic Dentists
3. Interior Designers / Architects
4. Premium Salons / Wellness Businesses
5. Premium Fitness Businesses
6. Wedding / Luxury Event Businesses

### Ideal Customer

Premium positioning + professional business presence + high-value services + poor/no website.
